AP Tracker - Graphing & Analytics Ideas

Here are some ideas for additional data that could be visualized to improve operational insights on the manufacturing floor:

1. Issue Resolution Times (Mean Time To Resolution - MTTR)
- Graph the average time it takes to resolve issues per machine, row, or shift.
- Useful for identifying bottlenecks or maintenance backlogs.
- Visualization: Bar chart comparing machines, or a line chart showing MTTR trends over the past month.

2. Issue Volume by Category/Status
- Track the number of issues categorized by their status (e.g., Tool/Die, Maintenance, Quality, Process Engineer).
- Visualization: Stacked bar chart or pie chart to see which departments are handling the most issues.

3. Machine Downtime vs. Uptime
- If the system tracks when a machine goes down and comes back up, graph the total downtime hours per week.
- Visualization: Line chart or area chart tracking downtime hours, overlaying a "target uptime" threshold.

4. Gamification & Leaderboards
- Track user engagement: Who is logging or resolving the most issues?
- Visualization: Horizontal bar chart showing the top 5 contributors (XP or issues resolved) for the current week.

5. Repeated Issues (Hotspots)
- Identify parts or machines that have recurring issues logged against them within a short time frame.
- Visualization: Heatmap showing Machines vs. Days of the Week to identify specific problem periods.

6. Shift Performance Comparison
- Compare the number of issues logged or resolved between Day, Night, and Weekend shifts.
- Visualization: Grouped bar chart comparing the shifts side-by-side over time.

7. Inventory Threshold Alerts (DOH Extensions)
- Extend the DOH chart to overlay a red horizontal "Critical Threshold" line (e.g., < 3 days).
- Visualization: The current DOH line chart, but highlighting data points in red when they dip below the safe threshold.
