Parrots are bright, social birds known for their curved beaks, vivid feathers, and remarkable intelligence. Many species can mimic sounds they hear around them, but they also use a wide range of natural calls to communicate with their flock.

In the wild, parrots often live in warm regions and spend much of the day foraging for seeds, fruit, nuts, and other plant matter. They tend to be curious and active animals, which is part of why they have fascinated people for so long.
