What is AP Tracker?

A real-time, multi-tenant issue tracking platform designed for manufacturing floor teams to log, monitor, and resolve press and machine problems as they happen.

Every plant gets its own isolated workspace with a visual floor map, live issue log, configurable status categories, photo attachments, and role-based access. Data syncs instantly across all devices via Firestore — no page refreshes needed.

Key Features

Everything your floor team needs to track issues from discovery to resolution.

⌗

Floor Map & Press Grid

Visual press grid with color-coded status indicators. See open issues, top statuses, and machine health at a glance. Filter by row or machine.

↔

Swipe-Driven Updates

Change issue status with a swipe — optimized for gloved hands on the floor. Touch and mouse gestures supported for fast triage.

◷

Full Lifecycle Tracking

Create, edit, resolve, and reopen issues with a complete event log. Every status change, note, and attachment is recorded as an append-only timeline.

◉

Photo Attachments

Capture and upload photos from the floor. Images are stored securely in Firebase Storage with metadata in the issue record. Lightbox gallery for review.

☰

Multi-Plant Management

Each plant is fully isolated with its own data, members, and configuration. Switch between plants seamlessly with per-plant data routing.

⚙

Custom Status Engine

Status categories are defined per-plant and loaded at runtime. Admins can add, reorder, or recolor statuses without deploying code.

⬇

Export & Reporting

Export filtered issue sets as PDF or XLSX. Multiple report formats (detailed, summary, timeline) for meetings, shift handoffs, and audits.

✦

Role-Based Access

Granular permissions per plant: Owners, Editors, and Viewers. UI adapts to role, and Firestore security rules enforce authorization server-side.

★

Gamification

XP awards, level progression, daily streaks, missions, weekly leaderboards, and badges. Motivates teams and surfaces top contributors.

💬

Messaging & Alerts

Plant-scoped conversations (direct and group), in-app toast notifications, and browser push alerts. Category-based alert subscriptions per member.

📋

Wiki & Notes

Per-press wiki pages with revision history. Mobile notebook with rich text, checklists, and photo attachments. Shared plant-wide wiki library.

⌛

Timers & Reminders

Per-issue countdown timers with expiry notifications and optional auto-escalation. Timer events logged to the issue timeline.

How It Works

From sign-in to resolution in four steps.

01

Sign In

Google sign-in with automatic plant routing. Demo and kiosk modes also available for onboarding and training.

02

View the Floor

The press grid shows every machine with live status colors. Tap a press to see its open issues, or browse the full issue log.

03

Track Issues

Create, update, or resolve issues in seconds. Add photos, assign statuses, and set timers. All changes sync instantly to the team.

04

Report & Improve

Export reports, review trends, and use gamification data to identify coaching opportunities and celebrate wins.
